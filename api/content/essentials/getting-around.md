## City buses side by side

What each city's transit website showed on **October 29, 2026**. All three charge **$3** for one adult ride, and all three need **exact change** if you pay cash, because drivers do not carry change.

| | [Fredericton Transit](https://www.fredericton.ca/resident-services/fredericton-transit/fares) | [Codiac Transpo](https://www.codiactranspo.ca/moncton-transport/fares) (Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview) | [Saint John Transit](https://saintjohn.ca/en/transit/fares-and-passes) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One adult ride** | $3, exact cash | $3, exact cash | $3, exact cash |
| **10 rides** | $27.50 (rider card) | $29 (10-trip pass) | $27.30 (Transcard) |
| **Adult monthly pass** | $85 | $73 (30-day pass) | $83.50 |
| **Student monthly pass** | $60, with student ID | $58 (30-day), for full-time students at a school in Moncton, Dieppe or Riverview | $73.70, with school ID |
| **University pass (U-Pass)** | For students at UNB, St. Thomas University and the New Brunswick College of Craft and Design, through your school | Use the student pass | Use the student pass |
| **Children** | Under 6 ride free | Under 6 free. Ages 6 to 11 living in Moncton, Dieppe or Riverview ride free with a U12 pass | First 3 children age 6 and under free |
| **Seniors (65+)** | $65 a year with the 65 Plus Club Pass | Student price, and free on Wednesdays | $61.85 monthly pass |
| **Ways to pay** | Cash, or tap your debit or credit card, phone or watch | Cash, a reloadable fare card ($4), or the Codiac+ app | Cash, Transcards, monthly passes, or SJTRides mobile tickets |
| **Plan your trip** | MyRide app, Google Maps or the Transit app | Codiac Transpo app | Transit Route Finder on the city website |

Good to know:

- **Fredericton:** a transfer is free for one way. Buy student and senior passes at City Hall, and student passes also at the UNB Student Union Building.
- **Moncton area:** a day pass costs $7. Students need proof of ID and enrolment for the student pass.
- **Saint John:** you need ID to buy student and senior fares in the SJTRides app. Some multi-ride fares go up on January 1, 2027.

## Between cities: bus

**Maritime Bus** runs between towns and cities in New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island, 7 days a week. It does not go into Quebec.

- Buy tickets online at [tickets.maritimebus.com](https://tickets.maritimebus.com/) or call 1-800-575-1807, any time.
- Discounts: 10% off a round trip, 50% off for children aged 5 to 12, and 80% off for children aged 0 to 4. There is no student discount.

## Between cities: train

**VIA Rail's Ocean** train runs overnight between Montréal and Halifax. In New Brunswick it stops in places such as **Bathurst**, **Miramichi** and **Moncton**. It does not go to Fredericton or Saint John.

Book on VIA Rail's website or call 1-888-842-7245. Choose Economy (a seat) or Sleeper Plus (a cabin with a bed and meals).

## Tips

- Keep exact change, or use a card or app, so you are not stuck at the bus door.
- Check the bus schedule before you go out in the evening or on a weekend, because buses run less often.
- Students: ask your school about a U-Pass or a student pass before you buy a regular monthly pass.
