The big supermarkets in Greater Moncton also carry a large selection of international foods.

**Marché Moncton Market**, 120 Westmorland St, is open every Saturday from 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. Its **weekday food court** (Monday to Friday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.) has cooks from around the world:

| Food court vendor | Food | Open |
|---|---|---|
| Veena's Kitchen | South Asian | Monday to Friday |
| The Latin Corner | Latin American | Tuesday to Friday |
| ShanGRILLa | Mediterranean-Canadian fusion | Wednesday to Friday |
| Little Saigon | Southeast Asian fusion | Thursday and Friday |
| Ming's Kitchen | Chinese | Friday |

The **Marché de Dieppe Market** is at 232 Gauvin Rd, Dieppe.

## Fredericton

The **Multicultural Association of Fredericton** lists these ethno-cultural stores:

- Friendship Convenience Store
- Mail n' Mart
- One Nation Market
- University Café and Market

It also lists **Victory Meat Market** and **Peter's Meat Market**, and the **Fredericton Cultural Market** and **Northside Creators Market**. Ask the association at [mcaf.nb.ca](https://mcaf.nb.ca/en/resources/) for directions and what each store carries.

**Fredericton Boyce Farmers Market**, 665 George St, is open every Saturday from 7 a.m. to 1 p.m., with more than 200 local vendors and an on-site restaurant.

## Saint John

**Saint John City Market**, 47 Charlotte St, Uptown, has food vendors under one roof. It is open Monday to Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturday from 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Sundays and holidays.

For international grocery stores in Saint John, ask the **Saint John Newcomers Centre** at [sjnewcomers.ca](https://sjnewcomers.ca/contact-us/).

## Tips

- Settlement organizations and cultural associations often know where to find specific foods, and when community food events happen.
- Store hours change. Call before you make a special trip.
