### Saint John

- The **Saint John Newcomers Centre** runs a **Community Hub** at 75 Prince William Street, Suite 100, Uptown, a space for community events and cultural celebrations, with special rates for ethnocultural groups. To find your community in Saint John, [contact the centre](https://sjnewcomers.ca/contact-us/).
- Groups that take part in Saint John's CultureFest include the **Indo-Canadian Society**, the **Asian Heritage Society**, the **Arab Cultural Association** and the **Ukrainian Association**.
- **PRUDE Inc.** has supported newcomers in Saint John for 45 years, with programs such as the Newcomer Woman Leadership Program and the Young Ambassadors Leadership Initiative for youth.

## Student groups

- **St. Thomas University:** the **International Students' Association (STUISA)**.
- **University of New Brunswick:** contact the **International Student Advisor's Office** at isao@unb.ca or 506-453-4860. The student unions in Fredericton and Saint John list their clubs and societies, and UNB Saint John has more than 50.
- Ask your school's student services or international office about cultural clubs on your campus.

## Multicultural festivals

| Festival | City | When | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Cultural Expressions Festival](https://mcaf.nb.ca/en/cultural-expressions-festival/) | Fredericton | June, for Canadian Multiculturalism Day | Multicultural activities all month, presented by local ethno-cultural groups. [cefnb.ca](https://www.cefnb.ca) |
| [International CultureFest](https://sjnewcomers.ca/international-culturefest/) | Saint John | August, in Uptown | Running since 2009: music and dance from 22+ countries, a cultural village of local associations, family activities and food from around the world. Questions: culturalfestival@sjnewcomers.ca, 506-642-4242 |

Fredericton also hosts the **Harvest Jazz and Blues Festival** and the **NB Highland Games**.
