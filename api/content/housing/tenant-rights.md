## Rent increases

- Rent can go up by **no more than 3% every 12 months**.
- Your landlord must give you **6 months' written notice**.
- Rent can only go up **once every 12 months**, and not in your first 12 months.
- A landlord can ask the province for up to 9% for major renovations, and the province decides.
- **Think an increase is unfair?** Ask the Tenant and Landlord Relations Office for a review **within 60 days** of getting the notice. It is free. Include a copy of the notice.
- A landlord cannot raise your rent to punish you for complaining.

## When your landlord can enter

Your landlord **cannot enter without notice**. It is an offence.

| Reason | Notice required |
|---|---|
| Viewing, inspection, or showing the home to buyers or new tenants | At least 24 hours |
| Routine maintenance or normal repairs | At least 7 days, in writing |
| A repair you asked for in writing | None, within 2 working days of your request. After that, 24 hours in writing |
| Emergency | None |

Repair visits happen between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m., not on Sundays or holidays, unless it is an emergency or you ask. You do not have to be home.

## Repairs

- Your landlord must keep your home **in good repair and fit to live in**, and make **emergency repairs without delay**: fire, flooding, major leaks, or a heating system that stops working.
- Other repairs must be done within a reasonable time.
- You must keep your home reasonably clean and repair damage you or your guests cause. Normal wear and tear is **not** your responsibility.

## Evictions

- Only a **Residential Tenancies Officer** or a **judge** can evict you. Your landlord cannot lock you out or keep or throw away your belongings.
- **Unpaid rent:** you first get a notice giving you **7 days** to pay. If you do not pay, a final notice gives you at least **15 days** to leave.
- You **cannot be evicted for making a complaint**. If that happens, apply for help within 15 days of getting the notice.

## Discrimination

A landlord cannot treat you differently because of your race, national origin, ancestry, religion, family status, source of income or other protected grounds. Filing a complaint is free, you do not need to be a citizen or permanent resident, and it is illegal to punish you for it.

## How to get help

1. Talk to your landlord first.
2. Put your complaint **in writing** and give it to your landlord. For non-urgent problems, give them at least **7 days** to fix it.
3. Still not solved? **Apply for help** to the Tenant and Landlord Relations Office. You get a confirmation within one business day.
4. The office may try mediation, then a Residential Tenancies Officer decides.
5. Disagree with the decision? Contact the Court of King's Bench within 7 days.

**Tenant and Landlord Relations Office:** 1-888-762-8600, Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Mail: PO Box 5001, Moncton, NB E1C 8R3.
