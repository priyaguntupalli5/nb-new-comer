## Average asking rents

What landlords asked for new rentals, on average, in **April to June 2026**: Statistics Canada's latest figures, checked on October 29, 2026. Prices are per month and usually do **not** include electricity, heat or internet.

| | Fredericton | Moncton | Saint John |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A room** | $660 | $680 | $660 |
| **1-bedroom apartment** | $1,430 | $1,290 | $1,230 |
| **2-bedroom apartment** | $1,670 | $1,460 | $1,530 |
| **3-bedroom apartment or larger** | $2,070 | $1,810 | $1,660 |

Figures cover each city and the communities around it, such as Dieppe and Riverview for Moncton. These are averages: you will find homes above and below them. Statistics Canada calls these experimental estimates.

## Making your budget work

- Add the cost of **electricity, heat and internet** if they are not included.
- Plan for the **first month's rent plus a security deposit** of up to one month's rent when you sign. See [how renting works](/housing/renting).
- **Sharing** a home or renting a room can cut your costs a lot.
- Rent can only go up by 3% a year once you live there. See [your tenant rights](/housing/tenant-rights).
- A price far below these averages can be a sign of a **rental scam**. Never pay before you have seen the home in person or on a live video call.
