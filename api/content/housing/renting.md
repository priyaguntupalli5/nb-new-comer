## The lease

- A **written lease** is highly recommended. Even without one, New Brunswick's Residential Tenancies Act and the **Standard Form of Lease** still apply.
- You and your landlord must **each have a signed copy**. The Standard Form of Lease is free online and at any Service New Brunswick centre.
- **Roommates on the same lease** are all equally responsible for the whole rent and for any damage.
- Most leases start on the **first day of the month**, which is usually the earliest you can move in.
- Ask what the rent includes. Electricity, heat, phone and internet are often **not** included.

## The security deposit

- A landlord can ask for your **first month's rent and a security deposit**, but **not** your first and last month's rent.
- The deposit can be **at most one month's rent** (one week's rent for a week-to-week lease).
- Your landlord must send the deposit to the province's **Tenant and Landlord Relations Office** within 15 days. The office holds it and mails you a **Certificate of Deposit**. Keep it.
- **Getting it back:** apply to the office as soon as you move out. Each tenant on the lease applies separately.
- Your landlord has **7 days** after you move out to claim part of it, only for unpaid rent, unpaid utilities, late fees, or cleaning and repairs. You can dispute a claim with evidence.

## What a landlord can and cannot ask for

- You do **not** have to give your **SIN** to apply for or rent a home.
- A landlord **cannot refuse you** because of your race, national origin, ancestry, religion, family status, source of income or other protected grounds. You do not need to be a citizen or permanent resident to file a complaint, and it is free.
- **Pets** are up to the landlord, but a landlord must allow a **service animal** for a tenant with a disability.

## Ending a lease

Give your landlord **written** notice with the address, the end date and your signature:

| Type of lease | Notice you must give |
|---|---|
| Fixed term (for example, one year) | None: it ends on the end date |
| Week to week | One full week |
| Month to month | One full month |
| Year to year | Three months before the end of the lease year |

- **Assigning or subletting:** your lease may say you need your landlord's permission. Check section 6 of the Standard Form of Lease.
- **Victims of violence** can end a lease early for their safety or their children's safety.
