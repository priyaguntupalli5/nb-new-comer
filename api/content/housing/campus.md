## Getting to campus

- **Fredericton:** students at UNB, St. Thomas University and the New Brunswick College of Craft and Design can get a **U-Pass** for city buses through their school.
- **Saint John:** Saint John Transit offers discounted passes for full-time UNB students.
- **Moncton:** Crandall recommends living near a bus route. See [Getting around](/essentials/getting-around) for passes in each city.

## Advice from the schools

- **Start early.** Landlords often advertise four to six weeks before the move-in date.
- **Do not book your travel to Canada** until you have somewhere to live.
- **See the home first**, in person or on a live video call, before you pay anything.
- **Get a lease** and a receipt for any deposit. The deposit can be at most one month's rent.
- Listings on school pages come from landlords, not the schools, so check them carefully. Read [Spot a rental scam](/#housing) and [how renting works](/housing/renting).
